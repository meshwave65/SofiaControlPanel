No public contribution form, amount selector, payment method, terms acceptance, receipt, or payment/reconciliation mechanism was found on the pages read. Therefore task 12.1 is paused/partial pending owner decisions and any private mechanism evidence.

The site and kit are not aligned as a live supporter contribution journey. The kit says current MWN cannot be transferred or converted externally; /mwn describes a proposed interface with an external economy and publishes a preliminary acquisition reference, albeit with caveats. The difference cannot be silently reconciled. No changes were made to either source.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Verified: public page text as extracted at the URLs above; presence of partner/validation messaging; absence of a visible contribution flow in the four pages; content of the supplied kit.

Not verified: authenticated/private supporter flow, payment processing, accounting, receipts, tax/legal treatment, data protection process, whether MWN functionality has changed, and whether the displayed acquisition reference is still current. Absence from the public pages is not proof that no private flow exists.
