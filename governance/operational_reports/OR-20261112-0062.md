# OR-20261112-0062 — Market price non-interference rule

- **Mission:** MISSION_0021
- **Task:** 21.5 — MWN price and economic parameter governance
- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Status:** SUBMITTED
- **Scope:** Record owner rule separating market-determined price from administratively governed parameters
- **Database/code changes:** None

## Owner directive

The owner established a mandatory rule for the transition to automated market-price formation:

> Once market-based price formation is automated, nobody — including the system owner — may manually or artificially alter the market price. The market determines the price; the system only accepts and applies it.

This is a stronger restriction than ordinary role-based authorization. It is not an administrator permission that can be granted or revoked; it is a system-level invariant for the automated-price operating mode.

## Governance consequences

1. After activation of automated market-price formation, no human role, including the owner/super-administrator, may directly set, edit, force, replace, or override the market price.
2. The administrative interface may display the current price, source, timestamp, freshness/availability, and event history, but must not expose a manual price-edit operation in that mode.
3. The system may ingest prices only from the approved market-determination mechanism and must validate provenance, integrity, timestamp/freshness, units, and applicable acceptance rules before applying them.
4. If the market source is unavailable, stale, inconsistent, or invalid, the system must expose that condition and follow a separately approved fail-safe policy. It must not silently substitute a human-entered price or fabricate a market event.
5. Any correction to a demonstrably erroneous source event must preserve the original evidence and be represented through an auditable correction/reconciliation mechanism; it must not become a discretionary manual price-setting channel.
6. Administrative authority over other parameters — for example UMS/MWN parity and bonus percentage — remains a separate policy domain and does not imply authority over the market-determined price.

## Mode transition and unresolved policy details

The prohibition applies **after automated market-price formation has been formally activated**. The transition itself requires a documented activation gate, including the approved market source/mechanism, validation rules, freshness limits, failure behavior, and evidence that the system is correctly consuming the market result.

The owner has not yet specified:
- the exact market venue/mechanism that determines the price;
- whether the source is an internal market mechanism, an external market feed, or a combination;
- the fail-safe behavior and any pause/settlement implications during source outage;
- the process for source-data correction without introducing discretionary price-setting;
- the activation authority and acceptance criteria for the irreversible no-manual-override mode.

These are open design questions, not exceptions to the owner's stated invariant. In particular, no emergency owner override is assumed or recommended.

## Relationship to the parameter governance panel

The panel must distinguish parameter classes and capabilities:
- **Administratively governed parameters:** e.g. UMS/MWN parity and bonus percentage, subject to explicit authorization, validation, versioning, and audit.
- **Market-determined parameters:** MWN market price after automated formation is activated; read/validate/record/apply only, with no human write capability.
- **Other automated or hybrid parameters:** classify individually; do not infer their authority model from the price rule.

The market price remains part of the broader parameter registry/history, but its writer policy must be technically constrained so that privileged human administration cannot bypass the rule. A UI-only disabled field is insufficient; the authoritative backend/database write path must enforce the invariant.

## Safety boundary

This OR records an owner directive and its architectural implications. It does not activate automated price formation, select a market source, change a price, alter permissions, modify schema/code, deploy, or perform financial operations. No Supabase data or production system was modified.
