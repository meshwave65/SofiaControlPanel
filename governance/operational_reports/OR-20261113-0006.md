## Corrected owner directives
1. UMS is never a tradable asset/pair in MW-Ex. There are no direct UMS transactions in the exchange.
2. Fiat purchases/acquisitions of UMS are prohibited, except for the separately governed Genesis UMS contribution/acquisition process currently being implemented. This exception must not be generalized into ordinary fiat-to-UMS exchange trading.
3. UMS remains an internal MeshWave accounting/utility balance. When authorized UMS value is used to fund a market operation, it must first be converted to MWN.
4. MWN is the mandatory bridge from the internal UMS economy to the market boundary.
5. MWN does not create fiat value by conversion alone. To fund an external-token purchase with fiat, MWN must be sold into an actual buyer/liquidity source; fiat is recognized as available only after execution and settlement.
6. A user-selected UMS-hybrid funding mode is a source-of-funds instruction, not permission to spend available fiat/MWN instead of the selected UMS source. The user's example requires the UMS path to be honored even if other wallet balances exist.
7. Other funding modes may combine existing fiat/MWN resources and UMS conversion to cover a target order; any UMS-derived portion must follow UMS→MWN and then the required MWN sale/fiat settlement path before it can fund a fiat-denominated purchase.
8. The user's BTC example: for a R$1,000 BTC purchase in UMS-hybrid mode, convert the authorized UMS amount to MWN; submit a MWN sell order; after the MWN sale is executed and fiat is settled/credited, use that settled fiat to buy BTC. Without sufficient sell-side liquidity, the downstream BTC purchase cannot be treated as funded or completed.
9. The owner described a mixed funding example in which R$500 of target funding includes R$300 represented by existing MWN and R$200 to be sourced by converting UMS to MWN. The exact internal allocation, sale sequencing and order labels must be captured in the order specification without silently assuming a different meaning.

## Operational model — invariants
- UMS is not listed, quoted, matched, bought, sold, transferred or directly traded in MW-Ex.
- No direct UMS/fiat pair and no direct UMS/token pair.
- No fiat-to-UMS acquisition through ordinary MW-Ex order flow.
- UMS→MWN conversion is an internal protocol event, separate from a market trade.
- MWN sale is a market order/event, separate from UMS conversion.
- Fiat proceeds become spendable only after confirmed execution and settlement under the applicable custody/ledger arrangement.
- The subsequent target-token purchase is a separate market order/event, funded only by settled fiat.
- Partial fills, cancellation, expiration, insufficient liquidity, fees, price movement and settlement failure must propagate through the composed operation; no downstream amount may be represented as completed before its funding leg is settled.
- Existing market governance priority for Meshwalker orders over MeshWave own/treasury orders remains mandatory.
- Genesis UMS acquisition is a separately governed exception and must not be represented as ordinary UMS trading.
