## Owner direction

Keep the exchange experience organized in separate navigation areas. Transactions belong in the dedicated **Ordens** area. Pending orders must receive visual priority; the execution history must include fully and partially executed orders.

## Implementation

- Added `src/pages/MwExOrdersPanel.jsx` as the dedicated Orders workspace.
- Routed the existing desktop and mobile **Ordens** navigation item to this workspace.
- Added separate **Pendentes** and **Histórico de execução** views.
- Pending view has a highlighted priority treatment and reserves space for status, executed quantity, remaining quantity and cancellation affordance when operationally supported.
- Execution history defines columns for market/side, order type, requested quantity, executed quantity, remaining quantity, status and timestamp. This accommodates partial fills without collapsing them into a generic completed state.
- Kept MWN/DIN as the priority market; BTC/DIN and ETH/DIN remain complementary.
- Other navigation areas remain separate and retain their existing placeholder/read-only status.
