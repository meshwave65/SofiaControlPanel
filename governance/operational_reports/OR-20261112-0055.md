# OperationalReport — OR-20261112-0055

## Objective

Implement compact whole-unit balance presentation across user wallet overviews while preserving accounting precision in detailed statements; remove unsupported fixed MWN/BRL valuation; begin real read-only market-data integration for the MW-Ex.

## Execution summary

1. Inspected `MeshwalkerWallet.jsx`, `MeshwalkerStatement.jsx`, `MwcWallet.jsx`, `MwEx.jsx` and their stylesheets. Confirmed the wallet overview formatted UMS with up to six decimals, MWN with up to eight, and the recent activity list repeated fractional amounts.
2. Updated integrated wallet overview and recent activity presentation to whole units. Updated the detailed statement's balance summary to whole units while preserving decimal precision on each detailed ledger entry.
3. Updated the UMS wallet summary (UMS validated and potential MWN) to whole units. Acquisition detail, referral details, and WhatsApp/payment message formatting were not changed.
4. Reduced oversized balance typography in the integrated wallet, UMS wallet and MW-Ex to a compact bold hierarchy.
5. Removed the fixed legacy fiat valuation `mwnValueBrl = 0.021605` from the user-facing integrated wallet and statement summaries. Replaced it with an explicit message that UMS fiat value is derived from verifiable MWN/fiat market quote and current `UMS_PER_MWN`.
6. Created `api/market-data.js`, a server-side read-only aggregation endpoint with 60-second cache headers:
   - CoinGecko: BTC and ETH quotes in BRL, 24-hour change and last update.
   - HG Brasil: Ibovespa and S&P 500, pending server-side API key.
   - Banco Central do Brasil SGS series 433: latest monthly IPCA observations.
7. Updated MW-Ex to poll `/api/market-data` every 60 seconds, display provider-backed quote values, variation indicators, source and timestamp/reference, and keep unavailable assets explicit. IPCA is described as a monthly macroeconomic indicator; acceleration/deceleration is not styled as a normal asset-price gain/loss.
8. MWN is explicitly shown without a public quote; no independent UMS market quote is fabricated. Historical chart remains unavailable until a verified market series is selected for an asset with observable trading data.
9. Added server-side environment variable names to `.env.example`: `COINGECKO_DEMO_API_KEY` and `HG_BRASIL_API_KEY`. Neither is configured in Vercel production yet.
10. Deployed through GitHub/Vercel integration. Final production deployment `dpl_A2vUd4S4h2pdPtRM5bd35rT9Uoxn` for commit `ba9eda247592ea8461290551ae82082f83b091c9` reached READY. Vite build passed: 203 modules transformed, built in 3.21 seconds. Non-blocking warning: main JS chunk 713.04 kB (gzip 207.90 kB), above the 500 kB advisory threshold.
11. Queried the live production endpoint `https://meshwave.com.br/api/market-data`. It returned real CoinGecko BTC/ETH quotes and the BCB IPCA monthly series. HG Brasil reported `not_configured` because `HG_BRASIL_API_KEY` is absent.
